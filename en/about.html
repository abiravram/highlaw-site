About Us

An experienced and reliable lawyer and notary, committed to high standards of service.

Adv. Abir Avram

Lawyer and notary, married and father of three daughters. Graduate of the Haifa Reali School (1990). In 1997 he completed his LL.B with honors at Anglia Polytechnic University (Cambridge), Chelmsford branch — today known as Anglia Ruskin University.

Adv. Abir Avram did his legal internship at the District Court and Magistrates' Court in Haifa between 1997–1998, including under the Hon. Judge Bracha Bar-Ziv and the Hon. Deputy President Judge Shulamit Vesserkrog. In 1998 he was admitted as a full member of the Israel Bar Association. From 1999 he worked as an associate at a law firm, and in 2002 he founded his own firm, which he has headed ever since.

Adv. Avram has extensive experience in real estate — representing contracting companies, buyers and sellers in sale transactions, and tenants and landlords in private and commercial leases — as well as representation before courts at every level and before other tribunals. Since 2009 he has been a licensed notary, and he is a member of the computerization committee of the Haifa district of the Israel Bar Association.
