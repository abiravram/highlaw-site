Accessibility Statement

The internet is today the largest repository of information for all users, including users with disabilities. In accordance with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, and the regulations enacted under it, we place great importance on providing equal access to the information on this site, in order to enable a better browsing experience for everyone.

We strive to ensure that our digital services are accessible to people with disabilities, in the belief that every person is entitled to live with equality, dignity, comfort and independence. To that end, we work to align the site with the recommendations of the Israeli Standard for Internet Content Accessibility and the international WCAG 2.1 (level AA) document.

What this site does

This site was rebuilt from scratch with clean, simple code, applying basic accessibility principles, including:

  • A clear, semantic heading structure, enabling proper navigation with screen readers.
  • A "skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
  • Full keyboard navigation, with a clear focus indicator.
  • Alt text for meaningful images on the site.
  • Color contrast and layout designed for comfortable reading, with a responsive design across devices and screen sizes.

We continue to work on improving the site's accessibility on an ongoing basis.
