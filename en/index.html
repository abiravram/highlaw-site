About Us

Our clients come first

Abir Avram Law Office and Notary was established in 2002, and has specialized in civil and commercial law, with emphasis on contracts, real estate, urban renewal (TAMA 38), construction defects, torts, social security, companies, inheritance law and execution.

We represent individuals and companies in a wide variety of legal matters, in Israel and abroad, before courts, labor tribunals, planning and building committees, National Insurance committees and various appeal committees.

Our areas of expertise

The firm specializes in civil-commercial law, and also provides full notary services.

Real Estate

Representing buyers, sellers, tenants and landlords in private and commercial transactions and leases.
